    How to play Rules & scoring

    Keep your promise

    Each player calls how many tricks they will take. The dealer calls last and cannot make the total calls equal the number of cards dealt. Choose a call with the buttons or number keys 0–9.

    Play a highlighted card by clicking it or pressing its numbered shortcut. Follow the led suit if possible; otherwise play trump if you have it. A Joker is always legal. The winner leads the next trick.

    A familiar house variant

    Trump is the undealt face-up card's suit. A turned-up Joker means no trump. The eight nine-card deals also have no trump. Jokers always beat ordinary cards; the last Joker played wins. A Joker lead has no suit, so the next players must play trump if possible, or any card.

    This preserves the original project's simplified variant: no Joker declarations, trump selection, or section bonuses. The 24 deals are 1–8, four 9s, 8–1, then four 9s.

    Every trick counts

    • Exact call: 50 × tricks + 50 points.
    • Call and take every trick: 100 × tricks.
    • Miss your call: 10 × tricks taken.
    • Call above zero and take none: −200.

    The highest score after 24 deals wins. Ties are shared.
